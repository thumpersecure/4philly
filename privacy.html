Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

4philly.net ("the Site") is a civic transparency tool that aggregates and displays publicly available Philadelphia rental license and Licenses & Inspections (L&I) data. This policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you use the Site.

1. Data We Display

All property, license, and inspection data shown on this Site is sourced from public government records (e.g., the City of Philadelphia's open data portals and L&I databases). This data is public record and is presented here for civic transparency and research purposes. We do not independently verify the accuracy of third-party public records and are not responsible for errors in the source data. You can independently verify any record shown on this Site directly at the City of Philadelphia's official property lookup: atlas.phila.gov.

2. Data We Collect From Visitors

We aim to collect as little as possible.

  • No account creation. The Site does not require sign-up, login, or personal profiles.
  • No sale of data. We do not sell, rent, or trade visitor data to third parties.
  • We keep no visitor logs. The Site is a static website. We do not run a server or database that records who visits, what addresses you search, or what you type into forms. The Right-to-Know request form is filled in on your own device and is never sent to us. We have no visitor logs or search history to hand over, because we do not keep any.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to see aggregate usage (page views, referrer, browser type, approximate location derived from IP). We use these reports only in aggregate and do not use them to identify individual visitors. Google processes this data under its own policies.
  • Hosting logs. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any host, GitHub may log basic technical information (IP address, timestamp, requested page). We do not receive or keep those logs.
  • Advertising. The Site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technology to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's ad code runs on the page and may read the page's visible content to choose ads. We do not give advertisers your searches or any information you enter. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses data from sites like this one, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Local storage. If you enter your own AI assistant API key, it is stored only in your browser's local storage on your device and is never transmitted to us.

3. Third-Party Services

Your browser contacts these third parties while you use the Site: the City of Philadelphia's public data services (ArcGIS and Carto), which receive the address or account number you search for; Google (Analytics, AdSense advertising, and reCAPTCHA, which scores traffic to deter automated abuse); and GitHub (hosting). If you add your own AI assistant API key, your browser sends your requests to that provider. Each has its own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review independently. We do not control and are not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the Site and its infrastructure, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or the Site's data practices can be directed to the site maintainer via the associated GitHub repository.

4philly.net is an independent, non-governmental civic research project. It is not operated, affiliated with, or endorsed by the City of Philadelphia or any government agency.
