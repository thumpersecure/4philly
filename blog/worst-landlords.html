Philadelphia's Worst Landlords: 8 Years of L&I Violations, Ranked

Two live rankings, pulled straight from the City's own open data — no hardcoded list, re-queried every time this page loads.

Published September 22, 2026

Philadelphia's Department of Licenses & Inspections logs every code violation it issues, tied to the property's owner of record. That data is public. It's just never been assembled into a ranking — until now. Both lists below query phl.carto.com, the City's open-data mirror of the L&I violations dataset, live in your browser.

Read this before the lists load. Ownership on these records comes from free-text OPA owner names, not a stable owner ID — the same person or company can appear under more than one spelling, and this page makes only a conservative, limited attempt to merge obvious duplicates. Government, institutional, and utility owners are filtered out, but the filter is a keyword list and won't be perfect. Treat every entry as a lead to verify against the live L&I / Eclipse record and Atlas — not a finding of fact about any specific person.

How This Is Built

  • Source: the violations table on phl.carto.com — the same L&I dataset behind every property lookup on 4philly.net, joined by opa_owner.
  • Window: violations dated within the last 8 years from today.
  • Filtered out: the City of Philadelphia, Redevelopment Authority, Housing Authority, school districts, universities, hospitals, churches, and other government/institutional/utility owners — this is a private-landlord ranking, not a list of public agencies.
  • List 1 sorts by total violation count, no property-count minimum.
  • List 2 restricts to owners of 2–10 distinct properties and sorts by violations ÷ properties.
  • Nothing is stored. Like the rest of 4PHILLY, this runs entirely client-side against the City's public API — no server, no database, no account.

What This Isn't

  • Not a court finding. A violation is an L&I citation, not a legal judgment — some are contested, resolved, or under appeal.
  • Not owner-verified. Names come from OPA records as L&I has them on file, which can lag ownership changes, sales, or corrective filings.
  • Not exhaustive. Carto is a mirror of L&I's live Eclipse system and can lag days to weeks behind.

Before relying on any entry, verify the owner and property directly against Atlas or a live 4PHILLY property lookup.
